Navigate with your remote

Up / DownScroll the page. Hold to keep scrolling.
OKClick where the pointer is.
Left / RightMove the pointer (also Up / Down when the page cannot scroll).
Ch +/−Scroll the page several lines.
Up at page topReach the toolbar and address bar.
MenuProtection and pointer settings. Back Go back or leave full screen.

Protection is on by default. Your browsing data stays on this device.
